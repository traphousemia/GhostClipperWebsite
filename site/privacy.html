Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how GhostClipper ("the App") handles information. The App is designed as a local-first tool: most of your media and the work performed on it stays on your computer. This document describes what remains local, what may be transmitted, and the choices available to you.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the GhostClipper desktop application for macOS and Windows and any associated account services. It does not cover third-party platforms you connect to, such as YouTube, Twitch, Kick, TikTok, or X; those services have their own privacy policies.

2. Information That Stays Local

The following generally remain on your device and are not uploaded to us by the App:

  • **Video files.** Media you download from a URL or import from a local .mp4 file is stored locally. Transcripts, detected moments, and rendered clips are generated and saved on your device.
  • **Transcription data.** Audio transcription is performed locally on your machine; the resulting text is not sent to us.
  • **OAuth tokens.** If you connect a YouTube channel, the authorization token is stored locally on your device. The App uses it to upload clips you choose to upload.
  • **License file.** Any local license file and the entitlements it represents reside on your device.
  • **Monitoring configuration.** The channels you choose to monitor and the rules for automatic clipping are kept locally unless you opt into sync.

We do not have access to your local video library, transcripts, or clips unless you deliberately send them to us for support.

3. Information That May Be Transmitted

  • **Account data.** If you create an account, we receive an email address and a hashed password, or an identifier from a sign-in provider you choose.
  • **Credit and membership data.** Purchases, credit balances, subscription status, and transaction records are processed by us and our payment processor so your license works and billing is accurate. Payment card details are handled by the payment processor, not stored by us.
  • **Optional cloud sync.** If you enable account sync, limited settings and entitlement information may be transmitted so your license and preferences work across devices.
  • **Remote configuration.** On launch, the App fetches a configuration file that may include feature flags, version information, or a kill-switch directive. This request may include an anonymous device or install identifier and the App version.
  • **Support communications.** If you contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL], we receive the messages and attachments you send.

4. Analytics

The App may collect limited, aggregated usage analytics such as feature usage counts, crash reports, and performance data to improve reliability. Analytics are intended to avoid collecting the content of your media, transcripts, or clips. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before collecting analytics, and you may disable analytics in the App's settings where available.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to provide and maintain the App, process payments, enforce entitlements, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and improve the product. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Retention

Account and transaction records are retained for as long as your account is active and for any longer period required by law or for dispute resolution. Support messages are retained until a request is resolved and for a reasonable period afterward. Local media, transcripts, and clips remain on your device under your control until you delete them; we cannot retrieve them for you. You may request deletion of your account data at [CONTACT EMAIL], subject to legal retention obligations.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal information we hold, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact [CONTACT EMAIL]. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You can also stop using the App and uninstall it at any time, which removes locally stored data from your device.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information under our control, including encrypted transport for transmitted data. No system is perfectly secure, and you are responsible for safeguarding your device, account credentials, and license files.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or the law. Updates will be posted with a revised effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the App or by email where available.
