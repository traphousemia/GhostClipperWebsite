Good to know

What can I do for free?

60 credits every month, full URL clipping, captions and vertical formatting. Free clips carry a small GhostClipper.app watermark.

What is a credit?

One credit covers one minute of uploaded video. A 3-minute stream costs 3 credits. Credits reset at the start of each billing month.

When can I pay?

Paid plans open when checkout goes live. Until then every account gets the free tier — no card, no charges.
